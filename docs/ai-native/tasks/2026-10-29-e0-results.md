## Recommendation (C0 vs S1) — evidence only, no policy chosen
1. **Supported:** the state‑std floor (S1) removes the right‑arm‑state shortcut reproducibly (3/3 seeds); C0 collapses to 0 % under tiny unit‑correct perturbations in 3/3 seeds. Any baseline that is meant to be deployed on a real robot with state noise should not stay at C0's normalization.
2. **Not established:** whether S1 costs task success. Point estimates: NOM ≈ equal, BOUND −10 pp (largest on B22), but CIs are ±20–40 pp with 3 seeds.
3. Suggested next step (needs your approval; not started): add training seeds for both arms (rough estimate ≈ 8–10 per arm to get a ±10 pp half‑width if between‑seed SD ≈ 10 pp, i.e. ≈ 5–7 more seeds/arm = 10–14 runs × ≈1.7 h ≈ 17–24 compute‑hours at current speed) **or** accept the BOUND uncertainty and test only the floor on held‑out data later. Alternatively an S2‑type intermediate was not part of E0 and is untested here.
