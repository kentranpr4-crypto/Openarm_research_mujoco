# Offline decision-utility evaluation — PROTOCOL (pre-registered; frozen before any ranking result)

Date: 2026-11-01. Status of everything produced: **DEVELOPMENT** (both datasets were analysed in earlier WM research), privileged-state WM inputs (simulation research, disclosed), no learned-model training, no I-2, no reserved seeds 31–33. Code: `eval/decision_utility_lib.py`, `eval/decision_utility_run.py`, tests `eval/test_decision_utility.py`. Freeze: `outputs/decision_utility/protocol_freeze.json` (hashes of code, this document, model checkpoints, Gate v3 freeze).

## 1. Question
Do frozen world models rank counterfactual action sequences correctly enough to choose a good one? A candidate = one executed action sequence among the 25 registered variants of an initial state (control, depth, timing, noise, speed, release_height; all passed through the same clip + slew safety limits). Candidates of a pose share the same initial simulator state.

## 2. Candidate sets, data, support
- Development sets: (a) the 31 held-out poses of the wm2 test splits (≈ 25 variants each, plan failures excluded); (b) the 50 poses of I-1. Poses with < 10 usable candidates are dropped. Both sets are reported separately.
- Physically valid ≠ producible by ACT: all variants are valid executed actions (clip + slew), but they are scripted-expert perturbations. An **ACT-reachability diagnostic** compares per-candidate action statistics (mean |Δa|, max |Δa|, mean |Δ²a|, minimum gripper command) with the 1st–99th percentile envelope of the ACT roll-out episodes of the dataset and reports the in-envelope fraction per family. Results are never filtered by it.
- Support reported: poses, candidates, success rate, poses with outcome variation (some candidates succeed, some fail), spread of the true cost, fraction of horizon ends where the box is settled, ties.

## 3. Prediction procedure (no future ground truth)
Autoregressive blocks of K = 20 steps from t0 = 4. Block 0 input = the TRUE current-state rows t0−3…t0 (privileged states; known at decision time) and the candidate's own actions (past 4 and future 20); later blocks use the model's own predicted rows as history and the candidate's next 20 actions. The rolled length is the largest multiple of 20 such that t0 + 20·n ≤ T−1 (≤ 19 steps short of the episode end; the box has settled at the true horizon end in the fraction reported). No label, outcome flag, variant id, family or intervention metadata reaches the predictor (input keys: S_hist, B_hist, C_hist, A_hist, A_fut, t, Y_e = 0, pose; tested by perturbing every ground-truth row after t0 and checking that the predictor inputs are bit-identical).
Models (all frozen): WM3-B, WM2-C0, post-hoc hybrid (ridge joints + C0 box/contact), ridge, MLP (action-conditioned), action-blind MLP and ridge (ignore the candidate actions), and a "box stays" persistence null; plus a hash-seeded random ranking and the oracle as references. Yaw handling variants for WM3-B and C0 (inference only): model yaw (default) and yaw = persistence (zero yaw delta fed back into the rollout). **Not run:** Huber yaw head and rotation-vector head — their weights were not persisted by E1-lite and re-training is not allowed in this phase (reported as PARTIAL for that item).

## 4. Fixed scoring function (lower is better)
J = d_xy + |z_end − 0.384| + 0.05·1[palm force > 0.01 N at any row] + 0.05·1[max rise < 0.05 m], with d_xy the distance of the box centre to the tray centre (0.44, 0.30) m at the last rolled-out row. Weights are physical-unit choices fixed here; **nothing is fitted**. The true J is computed from the true rows t0…t_end of the same horizon (same function). A candidate is "placed" at the horizon end when d_xy < 0.03 m and |z − 0.384| < 0.006 m (the task tolerances). Sensitivity to other weights is NOT part of the primary result; any such analysis would be labelled exploratory.
Tie-breaking: among candidates with J within 1e-9 of the minimum, the smallest SHA-1 of "pose_id|variant_id|tie" (deterministic, not biased towards the control variant). Ties at the top are counted and reported.

## 5. Metrics (unit = initial-state pose; macro mean over poses; percentile bootstrap over poses, 10 000 resamples, seed 0, 95 %)
Top-1 success (selected candidate's episode-level task_success) against the random-choice and oracle rates; oracle regret J_true(sel) − min J_true and normalised regret (regret / (median − min), undefined without spread); exact oracle match; pairwise ranking accuracy over pairs with |ΔJ_true| ≥ 0.01 (tied predictions count 0.5) and Kendall τ; accuracy by predicted-difference bin (pairwise calibration); risk classification at the fixed operating point "predicted placed" vs true placed (precision, recall, accuracy, AUROC of J for true failure with a pose-cluster bootstrap of 500 resamples); calibration (ECE, Brier) of the model's own p(placed) at the last block (ridge excluded: its event heads are not used); per-family ranking (control + one family at a time); paired differences vs the action-blind MLP and vs WM2-C0; decision stability under the yaw variants (fraction of poses whose selected candidate changes, Kendall τ between score vectors, regret difference); latency per candidate (one block, batch 1, one CPU thread) and per 25-candidate ranking.

## 6. Rules
No scoring-weight fitting; no adaptive scoring; matched initial states and identical candidates for every model; no outcome label in scoring; ties and failures reported; compute capped at 0.1 process CPU-hour counting the sanity-check run (136 CPU-s already spent; the main run is capped at the remainder; stop and report PARTIAL, never silently raised); results are exploratory development evidence, not independent validation; no artifact of this evaluation is committed without separate approval.
