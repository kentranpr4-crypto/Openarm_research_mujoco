Findings (exploratory): (1) **robust scale alone is a no-op** — scale_only is bit-identical to the original, as expected from the invariance of Adam to a constant loss rescaling; (2) **the Huber form on the wrapped residual is the operative factor**: huber_only reproduces the full arm to within 0.0002 rad everywhere (rotating 0.0401 vs 0.0399, stable 0.0138 vs 0.0140), so the geometric mask adds nothing on top of Huber for this head; (3) the mask alone helps only partially (rotating 0.0957 → 0.0786) and does not make G7 pass; (4) no arm beats ridge on rotating windows (best ratio 1.036–1.042, CI includes 1.0); (5) failure cases for both Huber arms: static relative ratio 3.10 / 3.09 (limit 3.0), static error 0.0093 rad against persistence 0.0028; per-group, they are worse than P0 in 96 % of groups on stable windows and in 27–29 % on rotating windows, worse than ridge in 44 % / 53 %; validation: all evaluable components PASS (static relative 2.25 / 2.28, verdict NOT_EVALUABLE only because orientation is not output by a yaw head).

## 5. Recommendation — full yaw training or decision utility?
**Do not train the trunk for yaw; move to a decision-utility evaluation.** Reasons: (i) yaw is not part of any task label (`task_labels` uses position, z and speed) and the existing box/contact/event predictions already pass the position-centred gates; (ii) the yaw problem is solved at head level by a robust (Huber, wrap-aware) loss at 257 parameters and ~0.07 CPU-h, with no mask needed — a full retraining adds cost (≥ 7 CPU-h) without a demonstrated yaw skill beyond ridge; (iii) the rotation-vector head gives modest orientation gains (6–8 % over ridge) but leaves 3-D hallucination on stable windows and absolute tumbling errors of ~0.56 rad, whose decision relevance is untested.
Proposed next step (needs approval; offline on frozen predictions, ≲ 0.1 CPU-h, no training): **pre-registered decision-utility analysis** — rank the 25 paired action variants of each initial state by the predicted outcome (palm contact, lifted, placed events; predicted final box error) and measure top-1 / pairwise ranking accuracy and outcome regret against the true outcomes, for WM3-B vs C0 vs hybrid vs ridge/MLP, with and without the orientation heads, on the development splits first and on I-2 once frozen. It answers whether the remaining orientation errors change any decision before spending more compute. Note: the paired multi-seed (≤ 13.5 CPU-h) does not fit the lower remaining bound (11.8 CPU-h).
