## Outcome-definition caveat (post-hoc diagnostic; frozen results unchanged)
Re-executing the 11 nominal branches labelled 'dropped' (bit-exact) shows **11/11 are successful carries reaching the tray before t=100**: box within 0.4–0.9 cm of the tray centre at t_drop 94–100, still held one step earlier (box–TCP 2.6–3.7 cm), z falling into the tray. The frozen rule “no drop = rise never < 0.02 m after reaching 0.05 m until t=100” therefore counts arrival at the tray as a drop and makes the outcome depend on the schedule (a delayed/shifted trajectory, e.g. c4, reaches the tray after t=100 and is counted a success). The 4 'not_lifted' nominal cases are marginal lifts (rise max 5.4–5.7 cm vs the 5 cm threshold). Consequently the frozen success rates (nominal 68.8 %) and the 25 % headroom are **not valid measures of grasp quality**; the mid-stratum nominal success of 44 % is mostly this artifact.
